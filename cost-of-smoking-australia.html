What smoking costs in Australia 🇦🇺

A pack costs A$40 (about $26.00). At a pack a day, here is the money and the time.

Last updated 27 September 2026

A pack of 25 cigarettes in Australia costs roughly $26.00, which works out to $1.04 per cigarette. A 2024 analysis from University College London put the cost of a single cigarette at about 17 minutes of life expectancy for men — so one pack is around 425 minutes, or 7.1 hours.

Read the bottom row again. Ten years of a pack a day in Australia is $94,900 and roughly 1077 days — about 3.0 years — of life expectancy. Those are averages across large populations, not a prediction about any one person. They are useful for the same reason a speed limit is useful: not because it tells you exactly what will happen, but because the direction is not in doubt.

What changes when you stop

Recovery starts faster than most people expect. The first items are measured in minutes, not years.

  • 20 minutesHeart rate and blood pressure drop
  • 12 hoursCarbon monoxide in your blood returns to normal
  • 2 weeksCirculation improves, lung function starts rising
  • 1 monthCoughing and shortness of breath begin to fade
  • 12 weeksLung function measurably increased
